🌸 MHS BLOOM — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 | Effective: May 2026

Our Core Principle: Your data belongs to you — completely. We are merely a tool that helps you use it. We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data.

1. Who We Are

MHS BLOOM ("the App") is a cosmetic reference application developed by MH-SYNAPTIX. The App provides dermocosmetic product information for educational and reference purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Contact: privacy@mhsynaptix.com

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account Data

  • Email address — for account creation and communication
  • Display name — shown in your profile
  • Authentication tokens — for secure login (stored encrypted)

2.2 Usage Data

  • Product views — which products you view (anonymized count for daily limits)
  • Favorites — products you save to your favorites list
  • Search queries — what you search for (anonymized, no personal identifiers)
  • Mode preference — Consumer or Professional mode selection
  • Language preference — your chosen display language
  • Theme preference — light, dark, or system theme

2.3 Subscription Data

  • Subscription status — free, trial, or PRO
  • Purchase tokens — provided by Google Play for purchase verification

2.4 Skin Profile Data (Coming Soon)

When this feature launches, you may optionally provide:

  • Skin type, cosmetic preferences, care style preferences
  • Geographic region (for climate-relevant recommendations)
  • Age range

This data is entirely optional, requires explicit consent, and can be deleted at any time.

2.5 Technical Data

  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous app usage patterns (screen views, feature usage)
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports and error logs for app stability
  • FCM token — for push notification delivery (if notifications are enabled)
  • Watermark number — a sequential number assigned to your account for content protection

2.6 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal health or medical data
  • We do not collect biometric data
  • We do not collect location data (GPS)
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or files from your device
  • We do not collect device identifiers beyond what Firebase provides anonymously

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalize the App experience
  • To sync your favorites and preferences across devices
  • To manage your subscription and free trial
  • To send you notifications (only with your permission, only about your trial/subscription)
  • To improve app stability and performance (via anonymized crash reports)
  • To protect our content (via watermark identification)

We never use your data for: advertising, profiling, selling to third parties, or any purpose beyond improving your experience within the App.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Data is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase) with encryption at rest and in transit
  • Authentication tokens are stored using encrypted secure storage on your device
  • Cached PRO content is encrypted locally using AES-256 encryption
  • All network communication uses HTTPS only
  • We implement Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorized API access

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — View all data we hold about you (in-app data management page)
  • Edit — Modify your profile and preferences at any time
  • Export — Download all your data in a portable format (Data Export feature)
  • Delete — Permanently delete all your data at any time (Account Deletion feature)
  • Withdraw Consent — Revoke any previously given consent
  • Object — Object to any data processing

When you delete your data, it is permanently and irreversibly removed. We do not keep backup copies of deleted data.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted accounts: All data permanently erased within 30 days of deletion request
  • Cached data: Local cache expires after 7 days automatically
  • Analytics data: Anonymized and aggregated, not linked to individuals after account deletion

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google) — Authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting, push notifications. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing — Subscription payment processing. Google Privacy Policy
  • Expo (EAS) — App build and over-the-air updates. Expo Privacy Policy

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties. We do not use advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.

8. International Compliance

🇪🇺 GDPR (European Union)

We comply with the General Data Protection Regulation. EU residents have all rights described in Section 5. Our legal basis for processing is consent (for optional data) and legitimate interest (for essential app functionality).

🇸🇦 PDPL (Saudi Arabia)

We comply with the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (نظام حماية البيانات الشخصية). Data subjects in Saudi Arabia have the right to access, correct, and delete their personal data.

🇰🇷 PIPA (South Korea)

We comply with the Personal Information Protection Act. Korean users have the right to access, correct, delete, and suspend processing of their personal information.

🇯🇵 APPI (Japan)

We comply with the Act on Protection of Personal Information. Japanese users have the right to request disclosure, correction, and deletion of their personal data.

🇨🇳 PIPL (China)

We comply with the Personal Information Protection Law. Chinese users have the right to access, copy, correct, and delete their personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

MHS BLOOM is rated for ages 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App or via email. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
